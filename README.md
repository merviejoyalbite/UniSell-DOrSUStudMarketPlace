# UniSell DOrSU

A student marketplace app for DOrSU students. You can buy and sell school stuff like uniforms, books, supplies, gadgets, and even food, whether it's new or used.

**SDG:** SDG 8 (Decent Work and Economic Growth)

**Event:** ITMSD1 Inter-Class Mobile Hackathon

**Section / Team:** ZeroEight

## Why we made this

A lot of us sell and buy school items in Facebook groups and group chats. Posts get buried, it's hard to tell if a seller is legit, and meeting up can feel unsafe. We wanted one place made just for DOrSU students.

## How it works

1. Sign up with your student account
2. Browse or search for an item
3. Tap Reserve or message the seller
4. Chat in the app
5. The seller accepts and picks a meetup spot on campus
6. Meet up and pay in cash
7. The seller marks the item as sold, and you rate them

There's no cart and no online payment. We kept it simple on purpose.

## Meetup spots

Library Entrance, Canteen, Admin Building, Gymnasium, Main Gate. Sellers choose which ones work for them.

## What we're using

- Flutter (Dart) for the app
- Firebase for login, database, and photos
- Figma for the design
