# Week 1 Validation

This is where we record who we talked to and what we learned. Everything here comes from our real interviews and questionnaire responses.

## Our Assumptions

These are the things we guessed before talking to anyone. We wanted to check whether they are true.

1. Students have trouble finding or selling school items like uniforms, books, and supplies.
2. Students don't fully trust sellers in Facebook groups or group chats.
3. Students would feel safer meeting at a campus spot.
4. Students would use an app made only for DOrSU students.

## Questions We Asked

1. Have you ever bought or sold school items (uniforms, books, supplies, gadgets) with other students? How did you do it?
2. Where do you usually look for these items, or where do you post them?
3. What's the hardest part about buying or selling this way?
4. Have you ever had a bad experience, like a no-show, a scam, or an item that wasn't as described? What happened?
5. How do you decide if a seller or buyer is trustworthy?
6. Where do you prefer to meet up, and what makes a meetup spot feel safe?
7. If there were an app only for DOrSU students, what would make you use it?
8. What would make you not use it?
9. Would you pay in cash at a meetup, or do you want online payment? Why?
10. Is there anything else you'd want in an app like this?

## What We Found

| Topic | What people said | How many said it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where they buy and sell now | Facebook Pages | 2 of 2 |
| Biggest problem | Scams and canceled orders | 2 of 2 |
| Trust and safety concerns | Buying and selling this way does not feel very safe | 2 of 2 |
| Preferred meetup | They prefer to meet in person | 2 of 2 |
| Cash vs. online payment | Cash on delivery | 1 out of 2 |
| Would they use UniSell? | Yes | 2 of 2 |

## Were Our Assumptions Right?

| Assumption | Right, partly right, or wrong? | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Students have trouble finding or selling school items | Right | Both named scams and canceled orders as their biggest problem. |
| Students don't fully trust sellers online | Partly right | Both said buying and selling on Facebook Pages does not feel very safe. Because of scams and accuracy of size, colors and others of the item might not same in the pictures. |
| Campus meetup spots feel safer | Right | Both prefer to meet in person. |
| Students would use a DOrSU-only app | Right | Both said they would use UniSell. |

## What We Changed Because of This

- Listings must use real photos. We plan to add a check that rejects AI-generated photos, so sellers can't use fake pictures to scam buyers or the picture would be labeled as Ai-generated photo if the seller use ai.

## Things We're Still Not Sure About

- Online payment: we are not sure yet whether to add it, because we are concerned about the safety of students.
- We only asked 2 students, so our results may not be true for everyone. We plan to ask more students.
