# UniSell DOrSU: User Personas

**SDG Alignment:** SDG 8, Decent Work and Economic Growth

These are preliminary, fictional personas we made to guide the design of UniSell. They represent the kinds of DOrSU students who might use the app. We still need to check them against our interviews (see WEEK1\_VALIDATION.md) and update them if real students tell us something different.

## 1. The Budget-Conscious Buyer

**Name:** Mervie Jane Albite| **Age:** 19 | **Occupation:** 1st-year college student | **Role:** Primary user (buyer)

### About

A first-year student who just started at DOrSU and needs a  PE uniform, textbooks, and school supplies. Money is tight, so she looks for second-hand items or cheaper options. He mostly uses his phone for everything.

### Goals

- Find uniforms, books, and supplies at a lower price.
- Check what's available quickly without scrolling through many posts.
- Buy from other students he can trust.
- Meet at a safe place on campus.

### Frustrations

- Posts in Facebook groups get buried and he doesn't know what's still available.
- It's hard to tell if a seller is real or if the item is as described.
- Some sellers don't reply or don't show up.
- Meetups in random places feel unsafe.

### Needs

- A place to browse school items by category.
- Clear photos, price, and condition on every listing.
- A way to reserve an item and chat with the seller.
- Campus meetup spots he can choose from.

### How UniSell Helps

UniSell puts school items from other DOrSU students in one place, with categories, search, and a Reserve button. He can chat with the seller in the app and meet at a campus spot like the Library Entrance or Canteen.

## 2. The Student Seller

**Name:** Edgardo Balayo | **Age:** 21 | **Occupation:** 3rd-year college student | **Role:** Primary user (seller)

### About

A senior who has a uniform, textbooks, and other school items he doesn't need anymore. He would like to pass them on to other students and earn a little extra money. He sometimes sells small items he makes or buys to resell.

### Goals

- Sell items he no longer needs without a hassle.
- Reach students who really need them.
- Earn extra money for his own school expenses.
- Pick where and when he meets buyers.

### Frustrations

- Many people ask "is this still available?" and then disappear.
- He has to repost the same item many times.
- Haggling and last-minute cancellations waste his time.
- He isn't sure if a buyer is a real student.

### Needs

- A simple way to post an item with photos, price, and condition.
- A way to accept or decline requests and set his meetup spots.
- A Mark as sold button so the item stops showing up.
- Ratings so other students can trust him .
### How UniSell Helps

UniSell lets his post an item in a few steps, choose which campus meetup spots work for him, and accept or decline reservations. When the item is handed over, he marks it as sold and buyers can rate him.


### How UniSell Helps

UniSell is limited to verified DOrSU students and uses public campus meetup spots. A report feature and moderation tools could be added later, depending on what we build and who is allowed to use them.
